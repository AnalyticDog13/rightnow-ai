An open-source Instantly alternative you host yourself

If you like how Instantly, Smartlead or lemlist work but want the software, the mailboxes' logins and every reply on your own server, rightnow-ai is built for that. Here is an honest look at what is the same, what is different and what you give up.

Free software, your costs

The software costs nothing and has no paid edition. On your own computer there is no server cost at all; for always-on sending, a small VPS (1 vCPU / 2 GB RAM is enough to start) is the only hosting bill. You still pay for your own mailboxes and domains, as you would with any cold-email tool.

What you give up

  • No lead database or enrichment: bring your own lists. rightnow-ai is not a lead generation database.
  • No done-for-you mailboxes, pre-warmed domains or domain purchase.
  • No large shared warm-up network: warm-up runs between your own mailboxes; you can add an outside service.
  • No autonomous AI agents: optional AI only drafts, and a human approves.
  • You run and back up the server yourself.

Smartlead alternative

Smartlead is a hosted service for the same job: sending cold email from many mailboxes and handling the replies. rightnow-ai is a self-hosted option for that job, with your mailboxes, leads and replies in your own database. The table above shows where the hosting model differs.

lemlist alternative

lemlist markets multichannel sequences, with LinkedIn and other channels next to email. rightnow-ai is email only: its sequences send email steps and nothing else. If email is the channel you need and you want to run it yourself, compare the two with the table above.

Migrating

Export your leads as CSV from your current tool, then use the CSV import in rightnow-ai: map the columns to names, companies and custom fields, and duplicates are removed. There is no direct importer from other tools. Connect the same mailboxes, set up warm-up and DNS checks, and rebuild your sequences in the campaign editor.

Questions

Is rightnow-ai affiliated with Instantly?

No. rightnow-ai is an independent open-source project with no connection to Instantly or any other email-outreach vendor.

Can I import my leads?

Yes, as CSV. Export them from your current tool, map the columns during import, and duplicates are removed.

Will my deliverability be the same?

It depends on your mailboxes, your DNS records and your sending habits. No tool can guarantee inbox placement.

What does it cost?

The software is free (MIT). You pay only for your own server, if you use one, and your own mailboxes and domains.
